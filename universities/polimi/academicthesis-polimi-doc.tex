\begin{abstract}
\noindent \opt{academicthesis-polimi} is the Politecnico di Milano plugin of
the modular \opt{academicthesis} class. It reproduces the three official
thesis layouts of the School of Industrial and Information Engineering
(Scuola 3I, template revision October 2021): the \emph{classical} thesis,
the \emph{article} thesis and the \emph{Executive Summary}; it also
provides the PoliMi \emph{doctoral dissertation}. You only choose
\opt{university=polimi} and a format; the package picks the base class, page
layout, colours, cover and headings.
\end{abstract}

  & \opt{article}, 11pt, two columns & \file{example-polimi-summary.tex} \\

  & \opt{book}, 10pt, 17$\times$24\,cm & \file{example-polimi-phd.tex} \\

\end{tabularx}

All formats are A4, except \opt{phd}: 17$\times$24\,cm by default, A4 as an option. The showcases, in the \opt{showcases/} folder of the
repository, are complete compilable examples: copying one is the fastest way
to start.

\section{Quick start}

\begin{lstlisting}
\documentclass[university=polimi, master, format=classical,
               language=english]{academicthesis}

\thesissetup{
  title         = {Title},
  candidate     = {Name Surname},
  idnumber      = {000000},
  degree        = {Xxxxxxx Engineering - Ingegneria Xxxxxxx},
  supervisor    = {Prof. Name Surname},
  cosupervisors = {Name Surname, Name Surname},
  academicyear  = {2025-26},
}

\begin{document}
\frontpage

\frontmatter
\begin{abstractthesis}[keywords = {first, second, third}]
  English abstract.
\end{abstractthesis}
\begin{abstractthesis}[language = italian,
                       keywords = {primo, secondo, terzo}]
  Abstract in italiano.
\end{abstractthesis}
\tableofcontents

\mainmatter
\chapter{Introduction}
...
\end{document}
\end{lstlisting}

hyphenation. Without it, the main language is used.

\section{The formats in detail}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\opt{classical}}{classical}}

As in \opt{Thesis\_classical\_format}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Page}: A4, 12pt, two-sided; inner margin 3\,cm, outer 2\,cm,
    top/bottom 2.5\,cm; 1.5 line spacing; no paragraph indent, space between
    paragraphs.
  \item \textbf{Cover} (\cs{frontpage}): Scuola 3I logo, title in blue,
    ``Master's Thesis in \textit{degree}'', ``Author: \textit{name}'', and at
    the bottom Student ID, Advisor, Co-advisors, Academic Year. Raggiera at
    the top right.
  \item \textbf{Chapters}: big blue number and bar, ``\textbf{1$|$} Chapter
    title''. Starred chapters (\cs{chapter*}, e.g.\ for the Introduction) are added
    to the table of contents and to the running header.
  \item \textbf{Running header}: page number outside, ``1$|$ Chapter title''
    inside, no rules. Chapter opening pages show only the page number, on the
    right.
  \item \textbf{Sections}: blue, numbered ``1.1.'', ``1.1.1.''.
  \item \textbf{Blank pages} left before a new chapter carry the raggiera;
    \cs{blankpage} inserts one by hand.
  \item \textbf{Front matter} in roman numbers (\cs{frontmatter}), main text
    in arabic (\cs{mainmatter}).
\end{itemize}

Typical order: \cs{frontpage}, \cs{frontmatter}, English and Italian
abstracts, \cs{tableofcontents}, \cs{mainmatter}, chapters, bibliography,
\cs{appendix}, lists of figures and tables, acknowledgements.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\opt{article}}{article}}

As in \opt{Thesis\_article\_format}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Page}: A4, 11pt, 2\,cm margins, no indent; page number
    centred at the bottom, no header.
  \item \textbf{Page 1} (\cs{frontpage}): no separate cover. A header block
    with logo, title, ``Master's Thesis in \textit{degree}'', ``\textit{author},
    \textit{student ID}'', a rule, then a narrow column (Advisor, Co-advisors,
    Academic Year) next to the \textbf{abstract}, and the \textbf{keywords}
    in a blue box. The text starts right after it on the same page.
  \item \textbf{Sections} instead of chapters: blue, ``1.'', ``1.1.''.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}
\documentclass[university=polimi, master, format=article]{academicthesis}
\thesissetup{
  ...,
  abstract = {The English abstract, printed on page 1.},
  keywords = {here, the keywords},
}
\begin{document}
\frontpage
\section{Introduction}
...
\appendix
\section{Appendix A}
\clearpage
\begin{abstractthesis}[language=italian, keywords={qui, le parole chiave}]
  Abstract in lingua italiana.
\end{abstractthesis}
\begin{acknowledgements} ... \end{acknowledgements}
\end{document}
\end{lstlisting}

The \opt{article} class has no chapters. A \cs{chapter} still compiles (it is
set as a section, with one warning) so that a classical document can be
switched over, but use \cs{section} in this format.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\opt{summary}}{summary} (Executive Summary)}

As in \opt{Executive\_Summary}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Page}: A4, 11pt, two columns 30\,pt apart, 2\,cm side
    margins, no indent.
  \item \textbf{Page 1} (\cs{frontpage}): a full-width block across both
    columns with logo, ``Executive Summary of the Thesis'', title, ``Master's
    Thesis in \textit{degree}'', Author, Advisor, Co-advisor(s), Academic Year
    and a rule; the two columns start right after it. Raggiera at the top
    right.
  \item \textbf{Following pages}: a blue box at the top with ``Executive
    summary'' on the left and the author on the right; page number centred at
    the bottom.
  \item \textbf{Sections}: blue, with tighter spacing than the article format.
\end{itemize}

The student ID is not printed, so it is not required here. As in the
template, the summary normally ends with a short essential bibliography.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\opt{phd}}{phd} (doctoral dissertation)}

As in the PoliMi PhD template (\opt{phdthesis.sty}, \opt{phdtitle.sty}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Page}: 10pt, two-sided, chapters on right-hand pages;
    17$\times$24\,cm with 2\,cm margins (top 2.3\,cm, 0.4\,cm binding
    offset), or A4 with \cs{polimithesissetup}\opt{\{paper=a4\}}. Times for
    text, Helvetica for headings.
  \item \textbf{Cover} (\cs{frontpage}): PoliMi seal, ``Politecnico di
    Milano'', department and doctoral programme in small caps, a rule, the
    title, ``Doctoral Dissertation of: \textit{author}'' on the right, then
    Supervisor, Tutor and Chair of the Doctoral Program on the left, and
    ``\textit{academic year} -- Cycle \textit{cycle}'' at the bottom.
  \item \textbf{Chapters}: fncychap ``Sonny'' style (``CHAPTER'' and a big
    number, the title between two rules on the right).
  \item \textbf{Sections}: bold sans-serif with a rule underneath.
  \item \textbf{Running headers}: chapter on even pages, section on odd
    pages, bold sans-serif, with a rule; page numbers centred at the bottom.
    Blank pages are completely empty.
  \item \textbf{Front matter} in capital Roman numbers (\cs{frontmatter}).
  \item Captions with a bold label and italic text; a \env{defn}
    (Definition) environment numbered by chapter; grey three-line drop caps
    with \cs{lettrine}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}
\documentclass[university=polimi, phd, format=phd]{academicthesis}
\thesissetup{
  title = {Title}, candidate = {Name Surname},
  department = {Department XXX}, phdtitle = {Doctoral Programme in YXYX},
  supervisor = {Prof. Name}, tutor = {Prof. Name}, coordinator = {Prof. Name},
  academicyear = {2025--2026}, cycle = {XXXVIII},
}
\end{lstlisting}

Not taken over from the template, on purpose: \opt{psboxit} (not in TeX
Live; it only served commented-out page-number boxes), \opt{fancybox}
(unused), and the template's \opt{glossaries} setup (its
\cs{glossarystyle} command no longer exists). Load \opt{glossaries},
\opt{bnf}, \opt{crop} (printer marks), \opt{listings} and so on yourself
if you need them.

\section{Colours, theorems and other commands}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \opt{bluepoli} (CMYK 0.4, 0.1, 0, 0.4) is defined in every format; in
    \opt{article} and \opt{summary} it is also available as \opt{bluePoli},
    the name used by the official templates. Use it freely, e.g.\
    \cs{rowcolor}\opt{\{bluepoli!40\}} (needs \opt{colortbl}).
  \item \env{theorem} and \env{proposition} environments are ready, with
    blue bold heads and italic text, numbered by chapter (classical) or
    section (article, summary). Further \cs{newtheorem}s use the same style.
  \item Figure and table caption labels are blue (bold in classical).
  \item \cs{blankpage} (classical): an empty page with the raggiera, not
    counted.
\end{itemize}

\section{What the package loads, and what is up to you}

Only what the layout needs is loaded, to avoid clashes:

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Format} & \textbf{Packages loaded} \\
\midrule
classical & titlesec, caption, setspace, eso-pic, transparent, amsthm \\
article   & titlesec, caption, eso-pic, transparent, amsthm \\
summary   & titlesec, caption, eso-pic, transparent, amsthm \\
phd       & caption, amsthm, fncychap, titlesec, tocloft (Times fonts) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The core class adds graphicx, geometry, fancyhdr, xcolor, amsmath, lettrine,
babel and biblatex.

Left to you, as in the ``add your packages'' section of the official
templates: \opt{hyperref} / \opt{cleveref} (the layout works with or without
them), \opt{amssymb}, \opt{bm}, \opt{float}, \opt{colortbl}, \opt{subfig},
\opt{tikz}, \opt{algorithm}, \opt{enumitem}, and the \textbf{bibliography}:
the package sets no style, so configure it in your preamble, e.g.\
\opt{biblatex-options=\{style=numeric-comp\}} as a class option, then
\cs{addbibresource}\opt{\{...\}} and \cs{printbibliography}.

\section{Common errors}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Y Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Message} & \textbf{Cause} & \textbf{Fix} \\
\midrule
Unknown format `classic' for university `polimi' & Typo in \opt{format=} &
  Use \opt{classical}, \opt{article} or \opt{summary}. \\
Missing required thesis metadata: `abstract' & \opt{format=article} without
  the abstract & Add \opt{abstract=\{...\}} (and \opt{keywords=\{...\}}) to
  \cs{thesissetup}. \\
Missing required thesis metadata: `idnumber' & No student ID (classical,
  article) & Add \opt{idnumber=\{...\}}. \\
format=phd is the PoliMi doctoral dissertation & \opt{format=phd} with
  \opt{bachelor}/\opt{master} & Use the \opt{phd} degree option. \\

  degree with another format & Use \opt{format=phd} (or leave \opt{format}
  out). \\
Missing required thesis metadata: `department' (or `phdtitle', `cycle') &
  PhD cover data missing & Add it to \cs{thesissetup}. \\
Warning: The base class `article' has no chapters & \cs{chapter} in article
  or summary & Use \cs{section}. \\
Warning: `french' \dots\ was not loaded by babel & A block with
  \opt{language=french} & Add \opt{other-languages=\{french\}}. \\

Compile with pdfLaTeX (the default). In Texifier/Texpad, set the typesetter

\section{For maintainers: how the package is organised}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{File} & \textbf{Role} \\
\midrule
\opt{academicthesis-polimi.cfg} & Read by the class \emph{before} it loads its
  base class. Declares the formats (\cs{DeclareThesisFormats}), the base class
  and default options of each format, no second page, and English + Italian as
  extra languages. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi.sty} & \cs{polimithesissetup}, required metadata,
  loads the packages of the chosen format, then reads that format's
  \opt{.def}. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-classical.def} & Classical layout. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-article.def} & Article layout. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-summary.def} & Executive summary layout. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-phd.def} & PhD dissertation layout. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-english.ldf}, \opt{-italian.ldf} & PoliMi wording
  of the core labels, plus PoliMi-only strings. \\
\opt{logopolimi3i.pdf}, \opt{raggierapolimi.pdf} & Logo and background,
  converted from the template's EPS. \\
\opt{logopolimiseal.png} & Round PoliMi seal for the PhD cover. \\
\opt{academicthesis-polimi-doc.tex} & This manual. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Rules to keep in mind when editing:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \opt{.def} files are written in \textbf{normal LaTeX syntax}
    (they are read with document catcodes), so spaces and blank lines behave
    as usual. The \opt{.sty} and \opt{.cfg} use LaTeX3 syntax, where spaces
    are ignored (write \texttt{\textasciitilde}).
  \item \textbf{Never load a package from a \opt{.def} file}: when that
    package ends, LaTeX switches LaTeX3 syntax back on and every following
    space is lost. Add the package to the format's list in the \opt{.sty}
    instead.
  \item Each \opt{.def} registers its own front page with
    \cs{DeclareThesisFrontPage} (for \opt{bachelor} and \opt{master}) and
    changes core pieces (page styles, acknowledgements, keywords) directly:
    the core has defined them before loading this package. Pieces shared
    by classical, article and summary live in
    \opt{academicthesis-polimi-common.def}.
  \item Metadata is read with the core accessors \cs{thesismeta}\opt{\{key\}},
    \cs{IfThesisMetaEmptyTF}, \cs{IfThesisListEmptyTF},
    \cs{IfThesisDegreeTF}, and labels with \cs{thesisstring}\opt{\{Key\}}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Adding a format}: add it to \cs{DeclareThesisFormats} and to the
base-class switch in the \opt{.cfg}, list its packages in the \opt{.sty}, and
write \opt{academicthesis-polimi-<format>.def}.

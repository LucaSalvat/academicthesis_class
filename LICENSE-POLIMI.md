## Origin

The layouts of this package are adaptations of the official LaTeX templates
provided by Politecnico di Milano:

- the thesis templates of the School of Industrial and Information
  Engineering (Scuola di Ingegneria Industriale e dell'Informazione,
  "Scuola 3I"), revision of October 2021: *Thesis classical format*,
  *Thesis article format* and *Executive Summary*;
- the PoliMi doctoral dissertation template (`phdthesis.sty`,
  `phdtitle.sty`) by J. Stevens and L. Fossati.

They are not original work of academicthesis. The page designs, colours,
cover arrangements and heading styles belong to Politecnico di Milano and to
the authors of those templates.

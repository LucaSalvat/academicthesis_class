\chapter{What this package does}

A Bocconi thesis opens with an official front page that the university
adds to the bound copy, followed by a dedication page. The rest of the
thesis is up to the student. \pkg{academicthesis} prepares those opening
pages correctly, and gives the rest of the thesis a clean, consistent
layout:

\begin{lstlisting}
\documentclass[university=bocconi, master]{academicthesis}
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Two ways to start a thesis}

The class option \opt{simil\_frontpages} chooses between them.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l Y Y@{}}
\toprule
& \textbf{\opt{simil\_frontpages=false}} (default) & \textbf{\opt{simil\_frontpages=true}} \\
\midrule
Page 1 & blank: the official front page goes here & the \emph{simil} front page, printed by the class \\
Page 2 & blank & blank (back of the front page) \\
Page 3 & the dedication, or blank & the dedication, or blank \\
Page 4 & blank (back of the dedication) & blank (back of the dedication) \\
Required fields & none & title, candidate, degree, supervisor(s), student ID, academic year \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

``Simil'' means a printed likeness of the official front page: useful for
a draft, a digital copy or a submission where the official page is not
added by the university.

\begin{center}
  \preview[0.32\textwidth]{bocconi-simil-cover}{Simil front page (page 1)}\hspace{1cm}%
  \preview[0.32\textwidth]{bocconi-dedication}{Dedication page (page 3)}
\end{center}

\section{What you get}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The opening pages} in the right order, with blank backs,
    numbered with letters so that they never clash with the front-matter
    page numbers.
  \item \textbf{The simil front page}, bilingual like the official one
    (``Relatore / Advisor'', ``Anno Accademico / Academic Year''), with
    the degree, major, title, advisor(s), candidate and student ID.
  \item \textbf{Front-matter blocks}: abstract, statement of originality,
    acknowledgements and dedication, in English, Italian or French.
  \item \textbf{Checks} when the simil front page is printed: a missing
    field stops compilation with a message naming it.
\end{itemize}

\section{How it is organised}

The class has a core shared by every university and one package per
university. With \opt{university=bocconi} the core loads the Bocconi
package by itself. Part~\ref{part:dev} of this guide explains the code.

\section{Class options}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{3.6cm} L{3.2cm} Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Option} & \textbf{Values} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\opt{university} & \opt{bocconi} & Selects the Bocconi package. Required. \\
degree & \opt{bachelor}, \opt{master} & Written on its own. Default: \opt{bachelor}. \\
\opt{simil\_frontpages} & \opt{true}, \opt{false} & Print the simil front page (\opt{true}) or leave two blank pages for the official one (\opt{false}, the default). Written alone, it means \opt{true}. \opt{simil\_frontpage} (singular) is accepted too. \\
\opt{language} & \opt{english}, \opt{italian}, \opt{french} & Language of the front-matter headings and hyphenation. The front page is bilingual whatever the language. Default: \opt{english}. \\
\opt{other-languages} & e.g.\ \opt{\{italian\}} & Other languages used by single blocks (Section~\ref{sec:blocks}). \\
\opt{biblatex-options} & e.g.\ \opt{\{style=apa\}} & Options for \pkg{biblatex} (Section~\ref{sec:bib}). \\
anything else & \opt{twoside}, \opt{12pt}, \dots & Passed to the \pkg{book} class. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Bocconi has a single layout, so the \opt{format} option is not used, and
no examination or copyright page is printed.

\section{The data of your thesis}
\label{sec:data}

All the data is given with \cs{thesissetup} in the preamble. With
\opt{simil\_frontpages=false} nothing is required: only \opt{dedication}
is used.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.1cm} L{7.6cm} c@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{Printed on the simil front page as} & \textbf{With simil} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\opt{degree} & After ``Corso di Laurea Triennale / Bachelor of Science in'' (master: ``Corso di Laurea Magistrale / Master of Science in'') & \req \\
\opt{major} & Under the degree, as written & \optional \\
\opt{title} & Title & \req \\
\opt{subtitle} & Under the title & \optional \\
\opt{supervisor} / \opt{supervisors} & Under ``Relatore / Advisor'' (plural: ``Relatori / Advisors'') & \req \\
\opt{cosupervisor} / \opt{cosupervisors} & Under ``Correlatore / Co-advisor'' & \optional \\
\opt{candidate} & Under ``Tesi di Laurea Magistrale di / Master of Science thesis by'' & \req \\
\opt{idnumber} & ``Matricola n. / student ID no'' & \req \\
\opt{academicyear} & ``Anno Accademico / Academic Year'' & \req \\
\opt{dedication} & Page 3, right-aligned italic (both modes) & \optional \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\begin{notebox}{Writing the values}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \opt{degree} is written without ``Bachelor of Science in'', which
    the page adds.
  \item \opt{supervisor} takes one name; \opt{supervisors} takes several,
    separated by commas, and the label becomes plural.
  \item Names are printed as written; the official page uses capitals,
    e.g.\ \opt{\{Prof. NAME SURNAME\}}, if you want to match it.
\end{itemize}
\end{notebox}

\section{Logos: \texorpdfstring{\cs{bocconithesissetup}}{\\bocconithesissetup}}

The simil front page carries two logos: the vertical ``Bocconi'' wordmark
on the left and the ``Universit\`a Bocconi Milano'' logo at the bottom.
The logo files are \textbf{not shipped} with the package: put the official
files (PDF or PNG) next to your thesis, and the page uses them. When a file
is missing, the page draws a text stand-in in the same place, as in the
preview above.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{Default} & \textbf{Effect} \\
\midrule
\opt{wordmark-file} & \opt{bocconiwordmark} & The vertical wordmark, 6.6\,cm high. \\
\opt{logo-file} & \opt{logobocconi} & The bottom logo, 4.3\,cm wide. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{lstlisting}
\bocconithesissetup{wordmark-file = bocconi-vertical,
                    logo-file     = bocconi-logo}
\end{lstlisting}

\section{After the opening pages}

From \cs{frontmatter} on, the thesis uses the core layout of the class
(the same as LUISS): chapter openings, running headers and the
front-matter blocks of Chapter~\ref{ch:blocks}. A dedication can be given
either with \opt{dedication} in \cs{thesissetup} (page~3) or with the
\cs{dedication} command later.

\chapter{Troubleshooting}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{5.2cm} L{4.2cm} L{4.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Message (abridged)} & \textbf{Cause} & \textbf{Fix} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Missing required thesis metadata: `\textit{key}' & \opt{simil\_frontpages=true} and a field of the page is missing & Add it; the message shows an example. See Section~\ref{sec:data}. \\
simil\_frontpages must be true or false & Another value, e.g.\ \opt{yes} & Write \opt{true} or \opt{false}. \\
No front page registered for university `bocconi', degree `phd' & The \opt{phd} degree & Bocconi offers \opt{bachelor} and \opt{master}. \\
University `bocconi' does not offer formats & A \opt{format} option & Remove it. \\
Text ``Bocconi'' instead of the logo & Logo files not found & Put the official files next to the thesis, or name them with \cs{bocconithesissetup}. \\
File `academicthesis.cls' not found & \LaTeX{} cannot see the class files & Keep the line that inputs \file{academicthesis-repo-paths}, or copy the files next to the thesis (Chapter~\ref{ch:install}). \\
Warning: `italian' \dots{} was not loaded by babel & A block with \opt{language=italian} & Add \opt{italian} to the \opt{other-languages} option. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{The early configuration}

The Bocconi package is the example of a university \emph{class option}:

\begin{lstlisting}
\DeclareThesisBaseClass { book } { }
\DeclareThesisClassOption { simil_frontpages } { false }
\DeclareThesisClassOption { simil_frontpage }  { false }   % alias

\bool_new:N \g__bocconi_simil_bool
\clist_map_inline:nn { simil_frontpages , simil_frontpage }
  {
    \str_case_e:nnF { \academicthesis_class_option:n { #1 } }
      {
        { true }  { \bool_gset_true:N \g__bocconi_simil_bool }
        { false } { }
      }
      { \msg_error:nne { academicthesis } { bocconi-simil-value } {...} }
  }

\DeclareThesisSecondPage { false }
\bool_if:NF \g__bocconi_simil_bool
  { \DeclareThesisCoreRequirements { false } }
\end{lstlisting}

\cs{DeclareThesisClassOption} takes the option away from the \pkg{book}
class (which would otherwise warn about an unused option) and records its
value. Without the simil page nothing is printed from the data, so the
core's own checks are switched off.

\section{The package}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \cs{ifbocconi@simil} mirrors \cs{g\_\_bocconi\_simil\_bool} for the
    layout file.
  \item \cs{bocconithesissetup} sets \opt{wordmark-file} and
    \opt{logo-file} (key family \opt{academicthesis-bocconi/layout});
    \cs{bocconi@wordmarkfile} and \cs{bocconi@logofile} hand them to the
    layout.
  \item With the simil page, \opt{idnumber} is added to the core
    requirements, and \pkg{eso-pic} is loaded for the logos.
  \item \file{academicthesis-bocconi-frontpages.def} is read with the
    document catcodes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The opening pages}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Command} & \textbf{Role} \\
\midrule

\cs{bocconi@dedicationpage} & The dedication (from \opt{dedication}) or an empty page, then a blank back. \\
\cs{bocconi@degreecourse}, \cs{bocconi@thesisby} & The bachelor or master wording, from \cs{IfThesisDegreeTF}. \\
\cs{bocconi@coursefont}, \cs{bocconi@titlefont}, \cs{bocconi@bodyfont} & Sizes measured on the official page (12/17, 17/20, 15/30\,pt). \\
\cs{bocconi@gapA}, \cs{bocconi@gapB}, \cs{bocconi@gapC} & The three vertical gaps of the page. \\

\newcommand\bocconi@frontpage{%
  \pagenumbering{Alph}%
  \ifbocconi@simil

  \else

\section{Strings}

The official page is bilingual, so both \file{.ldf} files hold the same
Italian\,/\,English text. They redefine the core keys
\opt{SupervisorLabel(s)}, \opt{CoSupervisorLabel(s)}, \opt{IDNumber} and
\opt{AcademicYear}, and add \opt{BocconiBachelorCourse},
\opt{BocconiMasterCourse}, \opt{BocconiBachelorThesisBy} and
\opt{BocconiMasterThesisBy}.
